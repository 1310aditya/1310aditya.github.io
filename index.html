Featured

2026 · arXiv preprint

Beyond Factual Grounding: The Case for Opinion-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Aditya Agrawal, Alwarappan Nakkiran, Darshan Fofadiya, Alex Karlsson, Harsha Aduri.

We show that contemporary RAG systems are biased toward objective, factual content — treating opinions and diverse perspectives as noise rather than information to be synthesized. We formalize this through the lens of uncertainty (epistemic vs. aleatoric), argue that opinion-aware RAG must preserve posterior entropy rather than minimize it, and present an Opinion-Aware RAG architecture with LLM-based opinion extraction, entity-linked opinion graphs, and opinion-enriched indexing. Experiments show +26.8% sentiment diversity, +42.7% entity match rate, and +31.6% author demographic coverage on entity-matched documents.

Selected work

The papers below were presented at internal Amazon publications. Titles, years, and high-level contributions are listed; product names, proprietary datasets, and implementation specifics are intentionally omitted.

  1. 2023 · Amazon internal publication

    Product Catalog Cleaning by Instruction-tuned LLMs

    Aditya Agrawal (1st author), et al.

    Studies whether instruction-tuned LLMs can detect and correct noise in large e-commerce product attribute data. Demonstrates that with carefully engineered prompts, off-the-shelf LLMs can match or outperform human annotators on certain catalog cleaning tasks (~91% noise detection rate), and outlines a cost-aware inference path that scales to billion-item catalogs.

  2. 2023 · Amazon internal publication

    Customer Preferences over Product Attributes for Personalized Interactive Recommendations

    Co-authored (3rd author).

    Self-supervised learning of customer preferences against explicit product attributes (rather than latent factors), enabling interactive, intent-aware recommendation widgets. Compares sequential vs. pooling-based models and shows ~20% F1 improvement over a heuristic baseline; finds that aggregate engagement signal often outperforms event-sequence ordering.

  3. 2022 · Amazon internal publication

    Personalized Content Targeting using Partial Brand Propensities

    Aditya Agrawal (1st author), et al.

    A self-supervised, multi-label model that learns customers' brand-level purchase propensities and uses them to target recommendation content — including a heuristic that extrapolates decisions to cold-start items lacking brand signal. Online A/B tests showed +27% widget engagement and contributed to $148M in incremental sales.

  4. 2022 · Amazon internal publication

    A Comparative Study of Traditional, Graph-Based, and Cross-Domain Models for Recipe Recommendation

    Co-authored.

    Evaluates eight recommendation algorithms (factorization, Bayesian, graph-based, cross-domain) for a highly subjective, preference-driven content domain. Bayesian Personalized Ranking performed best; deployment on a smart-display surface increased engagement +22% over a curated/heuristic baseline.
